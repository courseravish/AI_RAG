RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It is a machine learning approach that combines retrieval-based methods with generative models. In RAG, a model first retrieves relevant documents or information from a database and then uses that information to generate more accurate and contextually relevant responses or outputs. This technique is often used in natural language processing tasks, such as question answering and conversational agents, to enhance the quality of generated content by grounding it in real-world data.
