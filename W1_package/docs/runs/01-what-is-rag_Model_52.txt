RAG stands for **Retrieval-Augmented Generation**.

It’s a pattern for building AI assistants where the model **retrieves relevant information from an external source** (e.g., a document store, database, or search index) and then **uses that retrieved context to generate** an answer.

In practice:
1. **Retrieve**: Search for the most relevant passages to the user’s question.
2. **Augment**: Insert those passages into the model’s prompt/context.
3. **Generate**: The model produces an answer grounded in that retrieved material.

Why it’s used: it helps the model answer with **up-to-date or domain-specific knowledge**, can **reduce hallucinations**, and can provide **citations** to sources.
